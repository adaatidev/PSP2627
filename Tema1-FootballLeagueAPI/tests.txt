Why would a football league need communication between applications?
    A football league needs communication between applications because it has to know the teams and their players in their database.

Which application is the client and which is the server?
    In this case, the client is teams.py and the server is players.py

What is the difference between PUT and PATCH?
    The difference between put and patch are that put updates, for example, player but it doesn't change their id, because put needs 
    it to know who is going to update. While patch updates parcially an object, for example, player, it doesn't have to change all 
    the variables it can just change their age or their name (patch also needs the id to know who is going to update).
